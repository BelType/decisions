# A fragment announces its own size

**Status: Decided 2026-10-22, Executed 2026-10-22.** Convened by the second prediction of [*a box announces its own size*](a-box-announces-its-own-size.md), which broke on 2026-10-21: the ear is deaf to every divided block and to the whole composed mode. Ruled in conversation with Egildo, 2026-10-22; the name is the previous record's, carried one step down.

## Context

The rim's `size` kind watches the top-level originals and reads one only while it is drawn as itself on a built page. Since [*the first fragment is constructed*](the-first-fragment-is-constructed.md) (2026-09-15) a block cut across pages is never drawn as itself: the page shows a constructed first fragment, continuation fragments and, where both cuts fall in one block, a two-cut fragment (`root.js`, `firstFragmentClone`, `continuationClone`, `fragmentOf`), and the original is parked with no box. In the composed mode every block is drawn through a composed clone (`cloneFlowPiece`), and so is a block holding a nested fixed box. The previous record's Verdict measured it: a `<div>` of fourteen paragraphs divided from page one, one paragraph grown by 40 px by script, zero signals; the same growth in an undivided block, one signal and one resume. The manual draws 7 of its 25 top-level boxes as themselves. The ear hears the boxes that matter least.

A change of style reaches a fragment as it reaches the original: a fragment wears the original's classes and ids, and a sheet, an inline style the script wrote before the clone was taken, a face or a component inside the copy lay out again in the fragment. A change of the original's tree after the copy was taken does not reach it, and that is a different ear (below).

## Decision

**What is drawn announces its size: every fragment on a built page is observed, and a size it did not have when the run presented it is an edit at the page that shows it.** Four rulings.

1. **The unit is the piece on the page.** The rim observes, beside the originals, every fragment the root slots into a built page: the constructed first fragment, each continuation, the two-cut fragment, the composed clone, the clone a nested fixed box left. It observes a fragment from the frame the page is built in and lets it go when the page is rebuilt or unbuilt; the root is the one place that knows, so the root tells the rim, through the client, which pieces a page has. A fixed box's per-page copy and a running element's are not flow and are not observed.
2. **A fragment's expected height is the one the presentation gave it.** The first reading above zero the observer reports for a fragment is the run's own, kept beside the originals' table; every later reading is judged against it within the quarter pixel. Not the feed's table: the measure feed lays out the whole block and the continuation, and never the constructed first fragment or the two-cut fragment, which exist only at presentation, so the feed has no number for them and a second arithmetic for the ones it has is the fork the account ruling forbids. The engine's own rebuild of a page makes new fragments with new first readings, so its writes are filtered by construction, with no flag.
3. **The answer is a resume at the page, not at the box.** A changed fragment names its source box, as every signal does, and the page it stands on; the default action is one `run({ resumeAt })` at the entry of the earliest such page, in document order, converging past it as every resume does. A continuation that changed on page forty of a block that began on page three does not re-cut pages three to thirty-nine: the fragments on them did not change height, and a change there at equal height is the stated limit. The signal's `boxes` stay the source boxes, so a host that cancels sees what it sees today; the page rides beside them in the detail.
4. **The budget is the box's.** Three resumes per source box per epoch, whichever of its fragments spoke, then the last layout stands and the console names the box once.

**What this is not.** A change of the original's tree after the copy was taken, a `<details>` opened by script, a component that renders into the original late, reaches no fragment and is heard by nothing here. That is not a size but an edit with no host to compile it; its ear is a `MutationObserver` on the originals, answered as a patch of that box, and the difficulty is telling the engine's own writes from the script's. Parked under its own name, *a box announces its own change*, for a round of its own.

**Refused.** A table from the feed for fragments (above). Observing the page's flow container (it is clipped at the cut's extent and never changes). Observing the parked original (it has no box). A reading the engine takes itself from the fragment on presentation (the ear never measures; the observer's first report is the same number, taken by the browser, with no layout the engine forces). Hearing a fragment on a page the reader is far from (the page is not built; it is heard when the page comes near, the previous record's limit).

## Verdict

Built 2026-10-22 on `round/a-fragment-announces-its-own-size`. The door is a callback the client sets on the root, `onPieces(built, removed)`: `#buildContent` hands it every assignee that is a copy, each beside its source (`sourceOf`), and `present()` the nodes it takes out of the pages it unbuilds; the rim's `pieces` observes and lets go. The root answers two more questions, `pageHolding(el)`, the page a piece is slotted into, asked when the rim judges, and `startOf(k)`, the address page *k* begins at, which the client resumes at. The proof is `packages/essay/test/size.test.js` (nine tests: the two of the divided `<div>`, one per mode, written red before the fold, and the composed appended sheet turned from deaf to heard) and the hearing probe, P40 (6 of 6); the battery 26 of 26 in 11.1 min against `eaddf19`, every standing record reproduced, and the pin names the round.

1. **Red first, the broken prediction green — held.** Before the fold the divided `<div>` heard zero signals in both modes. After it, one signal, `{ boxes: [d], pages: [3] }`, one resume `{ from: 3, to: 8, converged: true }` (the restart one page before the piece's page, as every resume), the account equal to a fresh run's over the document carrying the style from the start, and pages zero to two byte-equal, in both modes. The growth is written on the paragraph and on its drawn copy, as a script that styles by selector writes it: written on the original alone it reaches no piece, the reader sees no change, and nothing hears it (the hearing probe, P40b), which is the parked ear's.
2. **The composed mode hears — held.** The appended sheet in the composed mode is one signal naming the box and the page its composed clone stands on, answered by a whole run (the conditions key refuses the resume), whose account equals a fresh composed run's on the document wearing the sheet. The previous test's assertion of deafness is turned around.
3. **The engine's own runs sound no alarm — held.** Case 82 as written, its one `<section>` now heard through eleven pieces, and unwrapped: a whole run, a paced run and an edit's resume, zero signals in all six (P40a). The manual under the editing tools, 301 pages and 301 pieces, cut, scrolled end to end and edited (`{ from: 1, to: 1, kept: 300 }`): zero (P40c).
4. **Windowing is not news — held.** The 137-page document, 6 pages built at first, 88 of its 400 paragraphs divided and drawn through pieces, scrolled end to end: zero.
5. **Every standing battery record reproduces, and the editing-tools suite is green — held.** 26 of 26 gates; the editing tools 105 of 105; the essay suite 205 of 205, the root suite 411 of 411, `npm run check` green.
6. **The cost is negligible — held.** Observing the manual's 301 pieces, 0.1 to 0.2 ms; the rim's callback at most 0.1 ms over the largest batch (28 entries); its judgement at most 0.1 ms. On the 400-paragraph document the callback at most 0.2 ms over 308 entries and the judgement 0.1 ms.
7. **Stated, not measurable — stated, and one clause measured wider than written.** A foreign change in the presentation's own frame and a reflow at equal height pass, as stated. The third clause, *a change on a page not built is heard when it is*, holds for an original and not for a piece: a page the idle slices built far from the reader is skipped by the browser, so its pieces read zero and have no expected height until the page first comes near, and a change made before then is their first reading (P40e: a divided paragraph on page 110 grown on its original and copies, zero signals before and after `reveal`).

**What the record did not foresee.** The first reading is the presentation's own only where the page was laid out before the change; on a page never near the reader the window is not one frame but every frame until the reader nears it (prediction 7). The signal's `pages` is `null` for a box heard as itself, which is what keeps an original's answer at its own address.
