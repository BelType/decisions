## Context

Every fault of the table, the float and the cell since 2026-08-03 was one kind of fault, met one shape at a time: a carried float (2026-08-03), its geometric remainder (09-07), every branch gets a place (09-21), a cell keeps its own window (10-06), *every content-bearing item makes progress* (10-08, case 91), and still ahead a cell holding blocks (case 95), the column (the brief), a float below the top level (case 96), a spanning cell (case 92). The tablet's The Flows reads css-break-3 §2.1 as *a parallel flow contributes geometry and nothing else*: the Stonehand offers no candidate inside one and reads no constraint from within one, and in genuine print a delegate fragments the interior. §2.1 says something narrower — fragmentation is performed independently in each formatting context, and a forced break inside a float affects the content outside it only insofar as it may increase the float's height. That is the channel between flows, and it says nothing about who fragments the interior. On screen nobody does, and the tablet's own last sentence of The Flows admits it.

**What the rivals do, read on 2026-10-08.** All three keep one object for what a fragment shows and where its flow resumes. Blink's break token has `ChildBreakTokens()`, one per child that broke inside: a table row lays every cell out with its own incoming token, each cell a block formatting context with its own orphans, widows, avoids and forced breaks, and the row's outgoing token is one child token per unfinished cell (`table_row_layout_algorithm.cc`); a float nested anywhere is reached because the token tree mirrors the box tree. Typst's `measure_auto_row` lays each cell out separately into the region sequence, takes the maximum frame height per region, then lays every cell out again into exactly those heights, so frame *n* of every cell is the row's frame *n* and a line cannot be shown on neither page (`grid/layouter.rs`). FOP's `TableStepper` merges every active cell's own element list into steps, each step a `TableContentPosition` holding one `CellPart` per cell — a start and an end index into that cell's list — so the part painted on page *n* ends where the part for *n+1* begins. Cicero mints the two separately: the address from geometry (`driver.js`' `placesOf`), the fragment from a clone and a clip.

## Decision

**One. A fragmentainer is one object.** It receives a slice of a flow from a resume address, measures it under its own geometry, and closes with an entry of its own inside the outer fragmentainer's interval. The page is that object with a page box's geometry and the main flow; a cell, a float's continuation and a column are that object with their own geometry and their own flow. The tablet's The Flows states it; the architecture's *The fragmentainer* says who it is and in what shape. The collapse test of 2026-10-06 said the page, the column and the cell are three objects; this record says they are one object in three relations — a series, a chain, a fan — whose differences are the foot and the order of the entries, which the cell's build has now tested before the column.

**Two. The resume record is a tree** (I2 amended). An entry's record is its main path and, under every box the cut passes through that holds parallel flows, one child address per flow, recursively; the flat list is the wire form and a projection, and `remainingPx` and the item mark are fields of a node. A voided entry voids its subtree. `placesOf` already walks the tree and flattens it; `carriedFlows` recurses through blocks; `fragments.js`' `itemsOf` consumes the tree and stops reconstructing nesting from `holds` and `directItems`. This is Blink's token tree, arrived at from our own `item` tag.

**Three. The interior of a parallel flow is adjudicated by the same walk, with the outer cut as its edge, and only its height crosses the wall.** *A cell is a flow of its own and a row is cut at one height; what a cell shows and where it resumes are read from one layout, the feed's, which every fragment of the table reproduces.* (Egildo, 2026-10-08, accepted verbatim.) Orphans and widows, avoids, forced breaks and monoliths inside a cell, a float or a column are read by the walk that reads them in the main flow, priced on the same make-up order with the flow's foot as the limit; a row is priced by the sum over its items of each item's own best verdict at that height; every content-bearing item makes progress; a forced break inside an item is consumed by the item's own walk and crosses as height only (P37c's shape); a height at which a content-bearing flow shows nothing is not a break point inside its row (case 91, §4.1: a cell with no padding offers no break above its first line). `walkOf` is reused and never read a second time.

**Four. A clip is not a mechanism, and conservation is a gate** (C10 amended). Since a fragment holds exactly its interval, a clip inside content hides a loss rather than preventing one, and the loss above was invisible to every gate because the words were in the DOM and below the clip. The page-box clip stays: it is the page rendering's own edge (S69) and a monolith's slice needs it. The tiling invariant — every rendered source text node's non-space characters on exactly one page — becomes a gate of the battery over the cases and the manual, native and composed, red on a gap or an overlap and naming the first five.

**What survives of [*a parallel flow contributes geometry and nothing else*](a-parallel-flow-contributes-geometry-and-nothing-else.md), and what does not.** The status grammar admits `Superseded by <name>` for a whole entry only, and a decided entry's text never changes, so that record's status is left as it stands and the partition is stated here. **Survives, all of it built and unchanged**: the geometry channel (the title's first half and the specification text under it); Decision One, that a box which is a parallel flow keeps exactly one candidate in the page's ranking, the Class A boundary before it, and that nothing inside one is a candidate *of the page's procession*; Two, the boundary priced at the previous in-flow sibling's bottom; Three, `edgeStraddler` skipping a parallel flow; Four, the carried path's `remainingPx` and the continuation built at it; Five, *does this page fit* judged on the in-flow bottom; Six, `Ledger.append` comparing `paths[0]`; Seven, the composed mode's refusal beside a float, a standing limit. **Does not survive**: the title read as a statement about the interior — *and nothing else* — and its two refusals, *Refused: touching the wall. A parallel flow's interior constraints … stay unread* and *Refused: widening the mechanism below the top level. A table cell is still not reached*. The first is what this record reverses; the second was reversed in part by *every branch gets a place* and *a cell keeps its own window* and is reversed whole here. The other two refusals there (no law moves; the grab still refuses a float's interior) stand: no law is *weakened* here either, I2 and I5 and C10 each gain a clause, and the grab's refusal of a float's interior for FORCING is unchanged until the inner walk consumes the item's own forced breaks.

**Refused alternatives.**

- **Keep the exceptions, one shape per round.** The record since 2026-08-03. Each exception was right where it was cut and the next shape arrived with a stranger's document; the cost is the pendulum *every branch gets a place* named on 2026-09-21, and this record is its second half.
- **Hand the interior to the browser by setting each flow in a fragmentation context of its own** (the browser's own multicol on a slice, or a nested measure). It is the oracle question one level down and a second measure context, and the cell needed neither: what a cell shows and where it resumes are read from the feed's one layout. It stays open for the column, where the brief's first question still lives.
- **Typst's flattening and re-layout into committed heights.** It lays the whole block out again on every spill; our feed is laid out once per pour and the fragment reproduces it by literals, which is the cheaper road *and* the one I5 licenses (a decision under conditions the browser reproduces).
- **FOP's absolute windows** (a cell's orphans and widows as absent break elements, never priced). It would be total where we sum shortfalls and would let an impossible cell overflow where C10 forbids loss; the cell record chose the sum, and the sum stays.
- **A flat list with a stronger tag.** What the `item` mark already is. Nesting reconstructed from path prefixes is the code this record removes; a tag cannot carry a child's child.
- **Repeated table headers.** Declined, and the declining is unchanged; the FOP penalty-width note is filed in the checklist's part four and not built.
