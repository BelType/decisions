# Cicero enters the seam, in beta

**Status: Decided 2026-08-25, Executed 2026-08-25.**

## Context

The standing ruling was "the seam waits until it renders *well*, not merely first" — Furniture proper, footnotes, performance — recorded in this package's CLAUDE.md, its README, and the composition table's own comment in `packages/cli/src/orchestrator.js`. On 2026-08-25 the engine was pointed, for the first time, at a real third-party document: the ZENTE operator manual (~4,000 words, 163 images, twelve author stylesheets), served from a scratch harness beside the battleground's venue server. It paginated in ~450 ms and lost no content — its own ledger accounts for 3,784 flow words across sixteen monotonically advancing pages — and it disagreed with Chromium's print by six pages for one arithmetically exact reason: the venue writes `margin-right: -10mm` **inside** `@page:right { @bottom-right { … } }`, and `readGeometryTable` hoists a margin-box's own margin onto the page box. 794 − 108.9 − (−37.8) = 723 against Paged.js's 576; 22 ÷ (723 × 989)/(576 × 912) = 16.2, which is the sixteen. A second fault surfaced in the same reading: a conditional `@page` variant *replaces* the base rule's margin-box set instead of merging it, so `:right` reported only `bottom-right` and the running footer would have been missing.

Neither defect was reachable from the corpus. Seeds are generated and cases are hand-authored, and neither writes a negative margin on a margin box inside a conditional page variant — which is the finding underneath the finding: **the venue's two families cannot see what a stranger's stylesheet does.** Egildo ruled the same session that the wait had inverted — the engine now learns faster inside BelType than outside it.

## Decision

**Cicero enters the composition table as `--engine cicero`, declared beta.** The wait's premise — that composability should follow quality — is refused on the evidence just gathered: the seam is not a reward for rendering well, it is the instrument that finds out how well it renders. Real documents arrive through BelType, not through the corpus, and the corpus is structurally incapable of generating what a stranger writes.

Beta means three things, stated so they can be checked. **It is opt-in**: `pagedjs` remains the default and nothing changes for a document that does not name Cicero. **It is honest about what it does not do**: `renderPdf` refuses, naming `--engine pagedjs`, on the `engine-site` precedent — the Press's genuine-print delivery is undesigned (checklist §6), and a paged engine that quietly printed through an unproven path would be the "green for a reason nobody can name" scar in a new costume. **Its render conditions are visible, never silent**: I5's DPR ruling is relaxed for the on-screen preview and the browser console says so on every run, because a decision taken at DPR ≠ 1 is valid for the screen being looked at and not for print (`measure.js`'s own words); the explicit-line-height half is satisfied by the engine's interface sheet declaring a root `line-height`, which sits before author sheets and loses every tie.

Refused, with reasons. **Publishing stays refused** — `private: true` holds, and the CLI reaches the engine through a lazy `import()` that names the absence honestly rather than through a package dependency, because a published CLI depending on an unpublished package breaks at *install* time for a consumer, which is the worst clock to break on. **A quality gate on the seam is refused** — there is no threshold that "renders well" names, which is what let the wait stand for three weeks past its usefulness. **Nothing inside `src/` moves**: the adapter lives in `seam/`, so the tablet-governed interior keeps the property its CLAUDE.md states — no dependency outward, BelType untouched.

## Predictions

Frozen on write, break-versus-hold decided in advance.

1. **The two ZENTE defects are geometry-table faults, not pagination faults** — they live in `capture.js` and touch neither the Ledger, the Stonehand, nor the Compositor. *Breaks* if fixing either requires an edit outside `capture.js`.
2. **The DOM-free suites stay green untouched.** *Breaks* on any assertion change in the four unit suites.
3. **The battleground's battery is unaffected**, because nothing in `src/` changes and the harness reads `src/`. *Breaks* if `npm run gates` next door moves any standing tally.
4. **The exporter's directory copy is currently non-recursive and will lose `hyphen-patterns/`** — a latent fault in `packages/cli/src/exporter.js` that no shipped `dirs` entry had ever exercised, because KaTeX's font folder is flat. *Breaks* if the export ships a working Cicero page without that copy being made recursive.

## Verdict

All four held, and prediction 1 held harder than it was written. The first document rendered *through* the finished seam — BelType's own `test/book.md`, eleven pages under Chromium — presented as thirty 12-pixel squares, and the cause turned out to be the ZENTE faults' shared root rather than a third one: `readGeometryTable` matches `/size:/` and `/margin-<side>:/` by regex against a `@page` rule's whole `cssText`, which the CSSOM serializes with the nested margin-box blocks inside it. `@page :right { … @bottom-right { … font-size: 9pt … } }` therefore yields `size = 9pt`, displacing the bare rule's `size: a4` by parity specificity: a 12×12 px page box and a −161.86 px content box, all of it inside `capture.js` and none of it touching the Ledger, the Stonehand or the Compositor. Two frontier entries collapsed into one, which is the better finding — and the sharper form of the same lesson, since a `font-size` inside a margin box is something every real document writes and neither a generated seed nor a hand-authored case ever had.

Prediction 4 was the incidental catch: `copyVendored` copied only a directory's top-level files, so `hyphen-patterns/en-us.js` would have been silently absent from every export — a miss with no error, found by the prediction rather than by a broken build, and fixed in the same round. Predictions 2 and 3 held by construction: the adapter added no line under `src/`.

**Unpredicted, and the round's best result: the refusal caught a BelType defect.** `test/index.md` did not render at all — Cicero refused with `@page for '(default)' has no explicit size`, degrading to the unpaginated document as designed. The cause was in BelType, not here: `shell.js`'s mechanics declare `@page { size: A4; margin: 20mm; }` as the standing default, and four lines above it a comment named the margin-box families with a star-slash sequence that closed the comment early, so Chromium's error recovery swallowed the rule. Every BelType document without its own `@page` had been silently taking Paged.js's letter/margin-0 fallback instead. Nobody noticed because Paged.js HAS a fallback; Cicero's I5 refuses to guess, and a refusal is a detector where a fallback is a hiding place. Repaired in BelType, whereupon `test/index.md` paginates to **six pages under Cicero and six under Chromium** — the first through-BelType agreement between the two engines.

The beta ships with the `capture.js` fault standing, deliberately. The seam's own plumbing is proved end to end — the client loads, the engine is entered after the compiler's client stages, the asset table serves and exports, `--pdf` refuses, and one document fingerprints identically under all three engines — and the fault is Cicero's own, recorded on the frontier with its arithmetic, owed a case before it is owed a fix.

## Consequences

The seam's ruling is retired from this package's CLAUDE.md, its README, `docs/repos.md`, and the composition table's comment; what replaces it is the beta declaration and the two named refusals (no PDF, not published). The two ZENTE defects are recorded on the checklist's frontier as design opens with their arithmetic attached, which is where a finding somebody must act on belongs — not here. The engine now has a second source of documents, and its shortcomings arrive as BelType bug reports; the battleground stays the place that says whether a fix moved a standing number.
