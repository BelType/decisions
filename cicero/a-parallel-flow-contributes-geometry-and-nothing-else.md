# A parallel flow contributes geometry and nothing else

**Status: Decided 2026-09-07, Executed 2026-09-07.**

## Context

An assessment of the engine against the rebuilt essay, read on 2026-09-07, found three defects that no standing row named and one weakening of a law, all in one organ. `cases/21-parallel-flows` — a 96mm float beside five declared-height slabs in a 70mm page area — printed **four** pages under the engine's native mode where Chromium prints two, and under the composed mode raised `the walk failed to advance at block 5, word 13` and produced no pages at all. The [checklist](https://github.com/egildo/beltype/blob/42b348b547d269a217a7f193042fe216386a4650/packages/engine-cicero/docs/css-paged-media-checklist.md)'s *Parallel-flow continuations* entry carried a doubt filed on 2026-08-20 asking whether it lagged round 07's build or named a narrower remainder; the assessment answered it in the second direction and then found three things neither the entry nor round 07's record had named.

The specification is unambiguous and the tablet already says it. css-break-3 §2.1: *"When multiple formatting contexts are laid out parallel to each other, fragmentation is performed independently in each formatting context."* The tablet's **The Flows**: *"the Stonehand offers no candidates inside a parallel flow and reads no constraints from within one: its interior is the oracle's business."* The [architecture](https://github.com/egildo/beltype/blob/42b348b547d269a217a7f193042fe216386a4650/packages/engine-cicero/docs/architecture.md)'s The Flows says the same in shapes. **None of it was built.** `isOutOfFlow` has been defined in [`src/stonehand.js`](https://github.com/egildo/beltype/blob/42b348b547d269a217a7f193042fe216386a4650/packages/engine-cicero/src/stonehand.js) since the sixth round and is consulted by `lastInFlowChild`/`firstInFlowChild` — the §3.1.1 propagation walks — and by nothing else. No candidate had ever been tested against it.

Four readings, each measured before anything moved:

- **`adjudicate` offers candidates from inside a parallel flow.** Its loop excludes `box.inline || insideMonolith(box)` and nothing more, so a float's interior offers Class A points between its children, Class B points on its lines and Class C points in its end gaps, all of them ranked beside the main flow's by the make-up order. On case 21 the trace is exact: page one's furthest candidate at or below the limit is a Class C end gap inside the rail's third paragraph, so the cut falls inside the float; page two cuts inside it again; the slabs follow on pages three and four.
- **A Class A boundary is priced at the previous sibling's bottom, and the previous sibling may be a float.** The boundary before the first slab was priced at y 400 — the rail's bottom — in a 264px page area, so it never fitted on any page.
- **A carried continuation keeps its declared block size.** `mutateToContinuation` consumes words and truncates leading decoration; it never reduces a declared `height`. The rail is `height: 96mm`, so its continuation is 96mm again with one paragraph in it. §5.1: *"Layout is performed per-fragmentainer, with each fragmentainer continuing progress from the breakpoint on the previous."*
- **The composed mode measures a block beside a float at the containing block's width.** `composeDocument` reads `avail` from the raw clone's own rect, which for a block laid out beside a float is not the width its line boxes have. On case 21 the slabs were composed at 366px where their lines have about 230.

And a fifth, one layer down, which is why the composed run threw instead of refusing: **`Ledger.append` measured I1 against the minimum over the previous entry's paths.** A carried flow sits behind the main path by construction, so the minimum let the float's 26 stand in for the main flow's 93 and admitted a main position of 34. The entry was written; the driver's C2 advance check threw one page later about a walk the Ledger had already recorded.

## Decision

**§2.1's independence is built, for a parallel flow that is a top-level block, and it is a reading of the section rather than an amendment to it.** Five changes, in four files.

**One — the Stonehand consults `isOutOfFlow` on every candidate.** A box that *is* a parallel flow keeps exactly one candidate, the Class A boundary before it; anything *inside* one offers nothing at all. The boundary before a float is kept rather than dropped because §4.1 names it: *"a float and an immediately-adjacent in-flow or floated box"* is a Class A pair, and dropping it would lose a legal break point.

**Two — a Class A boundary takes its keeps from one sibling and its position from another.** The keeps stay the immediately-adjacent box's, which is what §4.1's pair means and what keeps §3.1's *should* on floats honoured exactly where the sixth round left it. The position is the previous **in-flow** sibling's bottom border edge, because §2.1 says the float is laid out *beside* the content and its own extent is therefore not a place in the procession the cuts divide. **Refused: taking the float's own margin-box top**, which the assessment proposed. It reads later than the §5.2 convention this file states everywhere else — candidate positions are border edges and the margins between truncate — so it would price a cut against room that truncates. One rule, and the previous in-flow sibling's bottom is it.

**Three — `edgeStraddler` skips a parallel flow and its interior.** C10's remainder is a *painted* shell and The Flows requires a parallel flow's to be *live*; naming a float there hands one box to two mechanisms.

**Four — the carried path carries its geometric remainder, and the continuation is built at it.** I2 already has the word: *a cut through a box's own extent still carries a geometric remainder beside the path*. So each carried path carries `remainingPx`, the account carries it as a view literal, and `mutateToContinuation` writes it as the continuation's border-box height — **only where the source declares `height` or `min-height`**. An auto-height flow gets nothing: its content already is its size, and writing a height there would clip what the remainder never measured, which C10 forbids. The reading is the source's, because a detached clone has no computed style to ask.

**Five — the build loop judges *does this page fit* on the in-flow procession's bottom.** `report.flowBottom` is the maximum over every top-level box, the float included: right for *did anything overflow*, wrong as the thing a cut is driven from. Where the main flow finishes and a flow beside it has not, the entry's main path is the document's end (`posOf(blocks.length, 0)`) and the flows still running ride beside it, so the next page presents their continuations and nothing else. `pageIntervals` already presents nothing for a start at `blocks.length` and the C2 check already counts `next.b > cursor.b` as progress, so the shape needed no new representation. Closing the document there instead would be losing content at the edge.

**Six — `Ledger.append` compares `paths[0]`.** The main flow's path is distinguished for one real reason and the architecture already states it: *it bounds the interval*. That is what *flow is forward* is about. `voidFrom` keeps the earliest of all paths, where the earliest live flow is the right anchor. This was made and committed first and alone, because it turns the composed crash into a refusal that names itself — *entry position runs backward (I1)* — and because it pins an invariant the suite had no test for.

**Seven — the composed mode refuses to compose a block a float reaches into**, leaves its lines to the browser, and says so on the console every run. The Compositor reads one measure per block; a float shortens the line boxes it reaches into; neither composed check can see the difference, because fit-by-construction compares to the same wrong `avail` and the line-count cross-check measures the hinted clone, which has exactly the lines the hints force. This is the tablet's *uniform law, never uniform pixels* applied where a decision cannot be taken under render-identical conditions (I5), and it is a **standing limit rather than a defect**: the real fix is a per-line measure — `compose()` taking `availableAt(lineIndex)`, the DP state extended to (node, line) — which is the same state extension the essay's `lines-5` looseness needs, so it is one build and not two. A float's own descendants are unaffected: they lay out at the float's own width, which is what their rect reports.

**Refused: touching the wall.** A parallel flow's *interior* constraints — its own orphans, widows and forced breaks — stay unread, which is the one outstanding tablet amendment and a build rather than a wording. Case 21's split is geometric, so nothing here needs it, and the divergence P29 cells a and b record is unmoved.

**Refused: widening the mechanism below the top level.** A table cell is still not reached. That is round 07's own limit and this round changes nothing about it.

**Refused: laws.** No law and no corollary moves. I2's plural is what this builds; C10 is what it restores; C3's ranking changes only where a parallel flow is present; C1 is untouched, the grab having refused a float's interior since [*the grab descends*](the-grab-descends.md).

## Predictions

Frozen from the assessment's own claims, before `src/` was touched, each with its break criterion.

1. **The Ledger change alone turns the composed crash into an I1 refusal.** *Breaks* if it still throws C2, which would mean the crash had a second cause.
2. **Case 21 reaches two pages in the native mode once the Stonehand and the remainder are both in**, and neither alone. *Breaks* if one alone suffices, which would mean one of the two defects was not real.
3. **Case 21's `cicero` column agrees with Chromium's print as a partition, not merely as a count.** *Breaks* on a page-count agreement with a word-level divergence — that would be arithmetic coincidence, and the case's `why` would have to argue it.
4. **Every other case is unmoved.** No case but 21 and the tall-float scratch draws an out-of-flow box that straddles, and the assessment's own tripwire says the corpus draws none at all — so a moved case is the change leaking into in-flow adjudication. *Breaks* on any case whose result differs from the same run against the pre-round `src/`.
5. **`cases/acceptance.js` keeps its pinned 12 pages in both modes.** *Breaks* on any other number.
6. **The engine's DOM-free suite stays green and gains a test per defect.** *Breaks* on a defect whose test cannot be written without a browser — which would be a finding about where the defect lives.

## Verdict

**Prediction 1 held.** With `ledger.js` alone changed, the composed run of case 21 stops raising `the walk failed to advance at block 5, word 13` and raises `entry position runs backward (I1: flow is forward)`. The new `test/ledger.test.js` case fails against the previous source and passes against this one.

**Prediction 2 held, and the intermediate number is worth recording.** With the Stonehand change alone, case 21 goes from 4 pages to **3** in both modes — the third page carrying nothing but the rail's tail, because the carried continuation was still asking for its declared 96mm. With the remainder carried as well it is **2**. Neither change alone reaches two.

**Prediction 3 held.** All three columns print **2 pages**, and the runner's partition check reports no divergence at all between them: page one carries 83 tokens and page two 23, identically on `cicero`, `cicero-composed` and `chromium`. 106 tokens printed against the document's 106, 421 characters against 421 — nothing lost on any column.

**Prediction 4 held, and the check is the useful part.** The whole case suite was run twice on the same machine — once against this `src/` and once against the pre-round `src/` extracted to a scratch directory and reached through `ENGINE_DIR` — and the two failure lists are **identical, line for line**: 27 checks failing on cases 01, 03, 05, 06, 07, 09, 11, 12, 13, 15, 17 and 18 in both runs, before and after. **Every one of them is environmental and none is this round's**: each page-count failure hits Chromium's own column as hard as the engine's (case 03 declares 6 pages and every engine prints 5; case 13 declares 5 and every engine prints 4), and case 12's is a letter-spaced string the local font renders with spaces between the glyphs. The pre-round run could not reach past case 21 at all — the runner has no per-case `catch`, and the composed crash killed the process, which is [NEEDED-FIXES](https://github.com/egildo/beltype/blob/42b348b547d269a217a7f193042fe216386a4650/.claude/NEEDED-FIXES.md) §4.1 costing a measurement in the ordinary way.

**Prediction 5 held.** `cases/acceptance.js` passes at 12 pages presented and printed, against Chromium's native reference of 12, in the native mode and with `--composed` alike; folios and the ordered totality check both hold.

**Prediction 6 held.** 58 tests to **68**, all passing: three in `test/stonehand.test.js` over a plain-object stand-in for one measured page, four in `test/fragments.test.js` for the carried remainder, two in `test/ledger.test.js` for the two anchors, one in `test/compositor.test.js` for the refusal's overlap geometry. Every new test that pins a defect was run against the previous source first and fails there.

**The battery ran at the close of the rounds (2026-09-07), and no standing number moved for this round — checked by a counterfactual rather than by a tally.** The three gates whose tallies the run disagrees with — `native` (0–199), `composed-v2` (200–299 `--composed`) and `v3-native` (400–449) — were each run a second time against the **pre-round `src/`**, the tree at `1cce345` extracted to a scratch directory and reached through `ENGINE_DIR`, and the two runs' reports are **identical seed for seed**, verdict, note and page count alike: 164 agree / 18 legal-divergence / 11 unclassified / 7 theirs-illegal native, 84 / 12 / 4 theirs-illegal composed-v2, 42 / 6 / 1 unclassified / 1 theirs-illegal v3-native. So the argument above is measured rather than argued: the corpus draws no out-of-flow box in any era, and nothing it generates reaches a line this round moved. **The three disagreements with `gates/records.js` are identical before and after these rounds and are therefore not the engine's**; they are the machine's, the record is left standing, and the account is in the repo [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/egildo/beltype/blob/42b348b547d269a217a7f193042fe216386a4650/CHANGELOG.md) and in the [checklist](https://github.com/egildo/beltype/blob/42b348b547d269a217a7f193042fe216386a4650/packages/engine-cicero/docs/css-paged-media-checklist.md) §7. The pin is not rewritten — only a complete run with every gate green may write one, and `cases` and `suite` are red here on a font substitution.

**Too good to be true, checked.** Two things looked better than expected and one of them was.

The first was real: the composed column agreeing with the native one *token for token*. It does, and the reason is the refusal rather than the composition — every slab beside the rail is a block the Compositor declines to compose, so both Cicero columns are running the browser's lines on this document. That is honest and it is stated in the case's README, because a reader who took the identical columns as evidence that the composed mode had been proved on floats would be taking the opposite of what happened.

The second was not: the first cut of the refusal was too wide. It refused every block whose rect overlapped a float's, which includes the float's **own** paragraphs — laid out in the float's own formatting context at the float's own width, where `avail` is exactly right. Narrowed to a float that does not contain the block, and the case's numbers did not move, which is why it would not have been caught by any check the venue has.

**One thing was found and not fixed, and it is recorded here rather than acted on.** The whole case suite disagrees with its own declarations on this machine, on all three engines, in 27 checks — a Chromium-version or font difference between this environment and the one the cases were declared in. It is outside this round and outside the engine; it belongs to whoever next re-declares the case family, and the two identical failure lists above are the evidence that it predates this work.

Part one §4 gains a parallel-flows capability line; part one §5 gains the beside-a-float composition limit as a standing limit with its pins. The Design opens entry keeps its old text and gains the answer to the doubt filed on 2026-08-20 — it lagged the build in one half and was right about the other.
