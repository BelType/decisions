# The engine knows no view

**Status: Decided 2026-10-24, Executed 2026-10-24.** A design round with Egildo, 2026-10-24, at the end of the fragmented view's third phase (`prosemirror-fragview`, Codeberg, phases 1 to 3 merged that day, its plan in that repository's `docs/plan.md`). It closes the question the studio's *capriccio* of 2026-10-23 had left open, whether an editor state becomes mandatory for Cicero, and it decides where the ProseMirror adapter lives. The three doors it names and the sweep were built the same day on `round/the-three-doors` and merged into `main` with the battery green and the pin standing.

## Context

The studio experiment paginates a ProseMirror document with Cicero through an orchestrator of its own (`src/positions.js`, `src/patching.js`): the state is serialized into the frame's DOM, Cicero cuts it, and the editor reads geometry back over Cicero's pages. The *capriccio* of 2026-10-23 asked whether the copies could go, with slices pulled from the state instead, and said in passing that making a state mandatory was acceptable since a headless browser is needed anyway. From it came `prosemirror-fragview`: a view for ProseMirror whose document is rendered into a chain of fragmentainers, each a component that pulls content from a position and gives back the position where it had to stop. Its basic fragmentainer, `OverflowBox`, reads the browser's own overflow. Three phases proved the seam on the studio's generic schema: parity with `EditorView`, the chain mapped through transactions, a renderer whose fragments carry the engine's own words (`fragment`, `split`, `start`, `end`, `away`, `adjoin`) so Cicero's dressing applies to them unchanged.

Phase 4 of that plan was "the BelType fragmentainer": Cicero's page implementing `fill`, and, in the plan's words, "`stonehand.js` and `chase.js` become the component, `driver.js` becomes the view's chain, `root.js` stops owning the DOM". The round's first proposal was to build that adapter inside the engine, at `packages/engine-cicero/seam/fragview.js`. Egildo refused the shape: BelType's mechanism must stay agnostic enough to run as one round from the CLI into a PDF, with no editor state and less machinery, and the ProseMirror side should be a separate implementation that consumes the engine's general objects, the geometry capture, the sheet rewrite, the constructed sheets, the measure and the adjudication, and fills the gaps the ProseMirror way.

What the engine's exports show, read for this round. The objects a consumer would use operate on a document and on elements, none on the root: `readGeometryTable` and `rewriteDocumentSheets` on the document's sheets; `createMeasureContext`, `takeReport`, `adjudicate` and `adjudicateFlow` on a feed element and its report; `BeltypePage` with `applyConditions`, `setCounters`, `setStrings` and `setFlow`; `documentOpsOf`, which already takes its readers (`styleOf`, `textOf`, `attrOf`) as arguments; `settleReads`, which takes `run` and `blocksOf` as arguments. Three places assume the root: a page's content arrives through the root's named slot (`chase.js`, one level of forwarding, `#contentSlot.assign(rootSlot)`), `settleReads`'s `run` is the driver's whole-document run, and the counter state at a position is replayed over the root's light DOM. The demo of 2026-10-24 (`/Volumes/dev/alpha`, a plain ProseMirror editor on the fragmented view) found one more thing: a change of box geometry needs new containers, and the view keeps a container whose chain key survives a new state.

## Decision

**Cicero is a function from a document and its sheets to cuts, and it knows no view. A view that wants it consumes its objects through a separate package, and the two paths must agree.** Four rulings.

1. **No state is mandatory, and the root stays.** The *capriccio*'s concession is withdrawn. The CLI's one round, `beltype build --pdf` under Cicero when it leaves beta, and the HTML lane are the engine's primary path: `<beltype-root>` presents the Ledger's intervals as it does today, the rim answers late truth, the battery in `packages/essay` is the proof. Nothing in `packages/engine-cicero` imports ProseMirror or any editor library, and a sweep test over `src/` and `seam/` for the word `prosemirror` fails the build, as `test/host.test.js` sweeps core for `fs`.
2. **Three layers, one direction.** `prosemirror-fragview` knows no engine: a sweep for `beltype` and `cicero` over its `src/` fails its build. `prosemirror-beltype`, a new repository (`ssh://git@codeberg.org/egildo/prosemirror-beltype.git`, created 2026-10-24), imports both: it implements the fragmented view's `Fragmentainer` and `ContainerFactory` over Cicero's objects, and fills every gap the ProseMirror way, positions for addresses, slices for clones, the chain for the Ledger, the state for the source DOM. The engine's words registry (`data-beltype.js`) is the contract between its renderer and the engine's dressing; the renderer emits the words, the engine never learns who wrote them.
3. **The engine opens three doors, each with the battery as its proof and no behaviour changed.** A page takes content from whoever holds it: `BeltypePage` gains a way to be given a content element directly, beside the root's slot forwarding, with the three clips and the grid untouched. The counter and string state of a document is computed over readers the consumer supplies, which `documentOpsOf` already allows and which the record asks to be kept so. The reads loop runs over an abstract chain: `settleReads`'s `run` and `blocksOf` are its door, and a consumer whose `run` is "refill the dirty entries" must settle through the same loop, never a copy of it. Each door lands on `main` on its own, before `prosemirror-beltype` needs it; a door that cannot be opened without changing a cut is a finding for this record's successor, not a workaround in the adapter.
4. **One engine, two hosts, one answer.** The proof of `prosemirror-beltype` is not the studio's orchestrator. The same document is paginated twice, once by the CLI's round under Cicero and once through `FragmentedView` with `prosemirror-beltype`, and the two must agree: the same cuts, the same words on every page, the same folios, the same meant warnings. The showcase (`test/index.md`, 76 pages by default) and the specimen are the documents. Where they disagree, the engine's round is the reference and the adapter is wrong, unless the disagreement is a cut the engine's round itself gets wrong, which is a case for the battery.

**What this is.** The marginalization doctrine applied to the engine's own host: the view is one more thing BelType does not own. It is also the tablet's "a saw costs at the cut" read from the other side: the engine pays for judgement once, and whoever holds the page, the root or an editor's view, gets the same cut.

## Consequences

- `prosemirror-fragview`'s plan corrects phase 4: the root does not stop owning the DOM, the driver does not become the chain, and the BelType fragmentainer is `prosemirror-beltype`'s, built against the engine's objects. The plan's open questions are answered as the round answered them: `fill` is asynchronous for what the engine can await and `moved` carries what arrives after; the window estimate stays the view's until a measurement moves it; an entry carries positions and the `next` hint, never geometry; the names stay.
- The fragmented view gains a door the demo found wanting: a way to invalidate every container when conditions change, since a `@page` edit dirties every entry and every page's geometry with them.
- `prosemirror-beltype`'s own plan lives in its repository, phased as the fragmented view's was: the page as fragmentainer without reads, measured against the CLI round on the specimen; then reads and counters; then the studio adopts it and retires `src/positions.js` and `src/patching.js`.
- The three doors are entries in `.claude/NEEDED-FIXES.md` until built, and the sweeps are tests in both repositories.
- The record gains `Executed` when the doors and the sweep are on `main`. The two-host proof is `prosemirror-beltype`'s to run, and its result is recorded there and cited here.
- The doors and the sweep are built and on `main` since the merge of `round/the-three-doors` (CHANGELOG 2026-10-24, *The three doors*): `BeltypePage.setContent` in `chase.js` (measured by *given content*, P42), the readers in `document-state.js`'s header, the loop's assumptions in `reads.js`'s header with `hide` naming a scope, and `test/the-engine-knows-no-view.test.js`.
- A fourth door is open since 2026-10-25, found by `prosemirror-beltype`: the continuation dressing, `ensureContinuationDressing` in `packages/engine-cicero/src/dressing.js`, which the root now calls too (CHANGELOG 2026-10-25, *The continuation dressing is a door*).
- Four doors more since 2026-10-28, found by `prosemirror-beltype` (its `docs/found.md`) while it built its originals and its pages outside the root, each additive so that the CLI's calls are unchanged (CHANGELOG 2026-10-28, *Four small doors for a view*): `settleReads` compares a page site with the page values the run says it was laid out under (`laidFacts`), `runningBoxes` and `topLevelBlocks` take the element the boxes stand in and `runningPosition` is exported, `setMarginBox` takes a node as a part, and the client's handle carries the intake's geometry `table`.
- Five doors more since 2026-10-28, the driver's own readings a view had written again (CHANGELOG 2026-10-28, *The driver's closures are doors*), each a lift or an export with the driver calling it: `placeAt`, `pageNameDownFrom`, `gapAbove`, `processionBottoms` with `fitsPage`, and `pinTrack`. A sixth copy, the bound `neverCutInside` names, is kept: the driver's rule is an index over the whole intake and no function of a box chain.

## Refused

- **The adapter inside the engine** (`packages/engine-cicero/seam/fragview.js`, the round's first proposal). It would have put a view's vocabulary in the engine's package and made the one-round path carry it. Refused for the reason the first ruling gives.
- **A mandatory state.** Withdrawn with the first ruling. The one-round path needs a browser; it does not need an editor.
- **The fragmented view learning Cicero.** The generic view stays generic; one engine's words reach it only through its renderer's registry, which is the view's own convention.
- **The studio's orchestrator as the proof.** It is the thing being replaced, and two implementations of the same guess agreeing proves the guess, not the cuts. The CLI round is the reference.
