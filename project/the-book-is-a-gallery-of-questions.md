# The book is a gallery of questions

**Status: Decided 2026-08-19.** Executed by [the book has no PDF](the-book-has-no-pdf.md), which rebuilt the book to this unit on 2026-08-21. Formerly the entry numbered 004.

## Context

The book was written before the engine had its autonomous shape: organized around campaign history, speaking in numbered house rules, prose-only. Judged too inward for a venue built on demonstration.

## Decision

**The book's unit is one question, stated in general terms, with a standing anatomy:** what the specification says, what settled print craft says, what Chromium actually does with departures named, what we do and why, and the seeded proof.

An opening movement tells the pipeline story linearly — the journey from received HTML to the last page — and the gallery of questions follows.

**Readable throughout.** No house-numbered rules in the prose. Written for someone who has never seen the venue, with Paged.js, WeasyPrint and the others as reference comparisons rather than parents.

**Book structure and engine structure are the same shape on purpose.** The engine's side of the same cut is [questions and solvers](../cicero/questions-and-solvers.md): the engine is a set of questions, each with a solver, and the book is the gallery of the same questions.

## Consequences

The existing chapters are mined for material, not edited in place — the old organization *was* the jargon problem.

This entry decided the unit and nothing else; the rebuild that carried it out is its own decision, with its own medium, arc and frozen predictions.
