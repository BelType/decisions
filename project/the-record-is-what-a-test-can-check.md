# The record is what a test can check

**Status: Decided 2026-08-20, executed in three moves through 2026-08-21.** Carries the entries formerly numbered 005 (the process streamline), 007 (the executed round documents demoted) and 008 (the record checks itself). They were three decisions over two days, and one argument.

## Context

The process as written on 2026-08-03 prescribed a GitHub layer per cycle — paired pull requests with cross-linked bodies, twin annotated tags each naming the other, the venue's version minor encoding the last closed campaign — plus a four-document genre per round: pre-registration, campaign results, round document, changelog entry.

The protocol's own founding rule condemned all of it: **a device must be checked by something that can fail.** The pull requests and tags were checked by nothing. The live discussion they existed to cross-link happens in sessions and lands in files. The version claim was never once consulted. Both full executions showed the ceremony to be minutes of bookkeeping around days of real work, and the second ran without any of it and lost nothing checkable.

The same question then came for the documents the ceremony left behind, and for the record that replaced it.

## Decision

**Everything the project records is either checked by something that can fail, or it is prose that admits it.**

Three moves, in the order they were made.

**The GitHub layer is cut entire, and the document genre collapses into one decision record.** What survives is exactly the load-bearing half, each piece named by its check: branches, predictions frozen on write, the full battery, the content-addressed pin, the venue's generated scoreboard, and one changelog entry. The campaign-folder genre is retired and `campaigns/` is sealed history. Homes for unfinished findings drop from three to two — the checklist's frontier and a chapter's `wanted` list — because the third was the next campaign's pre-registration, which died with the genre. A decision entry is expressly not a parking lot: a document that proposes work is checked by nothing. Kept-versus-cut was weighed once more at the tag; "a name a person can say and check out" was the one residual use, and the round documents plus `git log` already carry it.

**The executed round documents stay on disk, demoted from reading matter to citation targets.** Pruning them into git history was proposed and refused on a blocker the sweep found: nine `src/` headers cite them by filename and item number, as do the venue's suites and runners, and the pin is content-addressed on the source tree — so those citations are frozen until a round moves `src/` for its own reasons, and files they point at must exist. What changed instead is what a fresh session meets: the orientation surfaces stop narrating them, the engine's own `CLAUDE.md` drops its round-by-round narrative and collapses nine table rows to one, and the reader is pointed at the book, which is the teaching layer. **If a future `src/`-moving round repoints those headers, the retirement refused here becomes free, and that round may take it citing this entry.**

**The record's mechanical half becomes asserted properties.** The genre was checked by nothing, which is the exact fault the first move cut the old ceremony for — and seven entries in, the status line already spoke four different grammars. So: a test, dependency-free like everything in the engine package, asserting the status grammar, supersession links resolving, Context/Decision/Consequences present, a verdict wherever frozen predictions ran, and every relative link resolving on disk. The four drifting status lines were normalized; the status is the genre's one mutable field, so this touched no decision text and *never rewritten* stood.

**Refused:** any retrofit of book pointers into existing entries — their texts are sealed, and one line in the record's README serves them all at once. And any check of what only a reader can judge: **one decision per entry, and the reasoning surviving, stay the README's demand and a reader's to enforce.**

## Consequences

The compound seam is stated once and holds: **the record keeps the moment, the book teaches the argument, the checklist holds the status, memory points.** An entry never grows a teaching paragraph the book already carries.

The next entry that drifts fails a suite instead of waiting for a reader. The venue's version number stops meaning anything beyond itself. A contributor arriving from the book meets tablet → architecture → checklist → decisions, and encounters a round document only by following a citation from the code or the suites — which is what an execution record is for.

The standing content of the round documents was verified already migrated before they were demoted: the checklist's rulings, its standing notes and its records section carry everything the rounds produced.
