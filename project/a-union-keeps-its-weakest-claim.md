# A union keeps its weakest claim

**Status: Decided 2026-11-20.** Egildo's ruling of 2026-11-20, "weakest claim", on the second of the two decisions [a union joins display lists, never finished files](a-union-joins-display-lists-never-finished-files.md) cannot make mechanically. Nothing is built.

## Context

A file claims what its `standard` asks unless a piece softens it. Under `unknownElements: 'infer'` a guessed tag withdraws a `ua-2` or `ua-1` claim ([the printer guesses, says so, and withdraws the claim](the-printer-guesses-says-so-and-withdraws-the-claim.md)), and lowers `a-2a` to `a-2u` ([a guess lowers PDF/A-2a to PDF/A-2u](a-guess-lowers-pdf-a-2a-to-pdf-a-2u.md)); a first heading that is not `H1` withdraws PDF/UA-1 ([a broken heading sequence withdraws PDF/UA-1](a-broken-heading-sequence-withdraws-pdf-ua-1.md)). The pieces of one union can soften differently, and a joined file makes one claim.

Two shapes were laid before him: the file keeps the weakest claim any piece kept, or the union is refused naming the piece that softened.

## Decision

1. **A union claims what every piece kept**: a claim one piece withdrew is withdrawn from the file, and a level one piece lowered is the file's level.
2. **The report and `X-Beltype-Standard` say so**, with the warnings of the piece that softened, as a single print says it.
3. **A refusal stays a refusal.** A piece that cannot keep an archival claim is refused as [an archival claim is kept or refused](an-archival-claim-is-kept-or-refused.md) says, and the union with it, naming the piece.

Refused: refusing the union where a piece softened. A single document is never refused for it, and a union would be harsher than one print.

## Consequences

- Asking for a claim is not a promise that the joined file carries it; a host that needs the promise reads `X-Beltype-Standard`, as for a single print.
